# Northwind Health Plan: Provider Bulletin 2026-07

**Subject:** Extended timely filing for HCBS providers

Following feedback from our provider network, Northwind is extending the timely filing limit for home and community-based services. For dates of service on or after **1 August 2026**, claims must be received within **45 calendar days** of the date of service. The 30-day limit continues to apply to earlier dates of service.

All other terms in the Provider Manual are unchanged.
