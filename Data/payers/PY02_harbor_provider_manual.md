# Harbor Medicaid Plan: Provider Billing Guide, HCBS chapter (extract)

**Payer ID:** PY02

## Visit verification
Harbor pays only for care supported by a verified EVV visit on the date of service for the same member and service. Only complete 15-minute increments of verified time may be billed. Partial units are not billable.

## Authorizations
Claims must reference the member's current Harbor authorization for the service billed. Units are drawn down as claims are paid; claims above the remaining balance are denied.

## Modifiers
Personal care (T1019) must be billed with modifier **U2**. Claims for T1019 without the correct modifier are denied.

## Daily maximum
A member may receive no more than **32 units per day** across all HCBS services combined. Claims that would take the member's paid total for the day above 32 units are denied.

## Filing limits
Harbor does not apply a timely filing limit to in-state HCBS providers.

## Duplicates
Harbor checks every claim against its claims history and denies duplicates.
