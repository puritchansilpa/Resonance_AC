# Northwind Health Plan: Home and Community-Based Services Provider Manual (extract)

**Payer ID:** PY01

## 4.2 Electronic Visit Verification
All personal care (T1019), respite (T1005) and attendant care (S5125) services must be supported by a verified EVV record for the member, service and date of service. Claims without one will be denied.

## 4.3 Units of service
Services are billed in 15-minute units. Northwind follows the 8-minute rule: after the last full 15-minute unit, a remaining 8 minutes or more may be billed as one further unit; 7 minutes or fewer may not. Units billed above what the verified EVV time supports will be denied.

## 4.5 Prior authorization
Every claim must carry the Northwind prior authorization number covering the member, service and date of service. Claims that exceed the units remaining on the authorization will be denied.

## 6.1 Timely filing
Claims must be received within 30 calendar days of the date of service.

## 6.4 Duplicate claims
Northwind denies any claim that duplicates one already received.
