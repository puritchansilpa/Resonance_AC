# Summit Care Partners: Network Provider Handbook (extract)

**Payer ID:** PY03

## Section 9: Claims for in-home services
9.1 Summit requires a verified EVV record for each billed date of service, member and service. Bill in 15-minute units of verified time; partial units are not billable, and units above verified time will be denied.

9.2 A Summit authorization number is mandatory on every claim. The authorization must be for the member and service billed and cover the date of service. Paid units are deducted from the authorization; claims exceeding the balance will be denied.

9.3 Claims must be received within 60 calendar days of the date of service.

9.4 Duplicate submissions are denied.

9.5 Additional billing requirements may apply under your agency's contract with Summit.
