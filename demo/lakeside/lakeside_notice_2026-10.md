**Subject:** Shorter filing window and holiday billing

From dates of service on or after **1 November 2026**, claims must be received within **60 calendar days** of the date of service. The 90-day limit continues to apply to earlier dates of service.

Personal care (T1019) delivered on a public holiday must be billed with modifier **U4**. A list of recognised holidays will follow separately.

All other terms in the Billing Manual are unchanged.
