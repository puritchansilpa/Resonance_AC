## 3. Visit verification
3.1 Every claim for personal care (T1019), respite (T1005) and attendant care (S5125) must be supported by a verified EVV visit for the member, service and date of service. Claims without one are denied.

3.2 Services are billed in 15-minute units. Lakeside follows the 8-minute rule: after the last full 15-minute unit, a remaining 8 minutes or more counts as one further unit. Units above the verified visit time will be denied.

## 4. Authorizations
4.1 Each claim must carry the Lakeside authorization number for the member and service, valid on the date of service.

4.2 Paid claims draw down the authorization. Claims that exceed the remaining balance are denied.

## 5. Coding
5.1 Attendant care (S5125) must be billed with modifier U1.

5.2 Respite (T1005) may not exceed 24 units for a member on any one day.

## 6. Submission
6.1 Claims must be received within 90 calendar days of the date of service.

6.2 Lakeside rejects duplicate claims for the same member, service and date of service.

6.3 Claims for members enrolled in the Lakeside Choice program may be subject to additional review.
